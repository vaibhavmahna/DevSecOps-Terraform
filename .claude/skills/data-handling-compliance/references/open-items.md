## Open items

Questions your organisation has not settled yet. List them here so agents FLAG them rather than guess. Remove an item once it is decided, and record the decision in the changelog below.

Typical examples to fill in for your own setup:

- **Retention period.** The end-to-end figure every store must meet, and any per-contract overrides.
- **Policy alignment.** Whether the privacy policy, terms of service and DPA all state the same retention and deletion commitments.
- **EU AI Act classification** of existing AI features. A Legal determination.
- **Backup rotation.** The actual figure for each system, so the deletion commitment can be stated accurately.
- **Regions.** Where personal and customer data may be stored and processed.

## Changelog

- Record each decision here with its date and who made it, for example: "Retention set at N days end to end, hard delete complete by day M."
